Follow this quickstart guide to configure your butcher shop, set up your species catalog, and process your first wild game order.

## Prerequisites

Before creating orders, complete the initial shop setup:

* An active Wild Game Butcher store account.
* Store business details, logo, and physical address.
* Your state tax rate and business tax ID.
* List of wild game species you accept and custom service rates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure shop profile and branding">
    Navigate to **My Store Settings** in the dashboard. Upload your shop logo, set your physical address for customer receipts, and enter your sales tax percentages in **Store Tax Details**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up species and compliance flags">
    Open **Species Settings** > **Species**. Enable the wild game species you handle (such as White-tailed Deer, Elk, or Wild Boar). Configure mandatory compliance flags:
    * **License Flag**: Requires customer hunting license number during intake.
    * **Permit Flag**: Requires game harvest permit or tag verification.
    * **CITES Flag**: Enables federal documentation tracking for regulated species.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure services and processing rates">
    Go to **Species Settings** > **Services** and **Species-Service**. Define your base services:
    * Standard processing (skinning, hanging, butchering).
    * Specialty caping and European mount preparation for taxidermists.
    * Assign per-head, per-pound, or fixed pricing models.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Define product cuts, add-ons, and packaging">
    Open **Product Settings** to establish your standard cuts and specialty recipes:
    * **Products**: Steaks, roasts, burger grind, snack sticks, and summer sausages.
    * **Add-ons**: High-temp cheddar, jalapeño mix, and pork or beef fat blends.
    * **Packaging Types**: Vacuum sealing, butcher freezer paper, and bulk packaging.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Intake a customer and create your first order">
    Click **New Order** from the top navigation bar. Follow the 6-step intake wizard:
    1. Search for an existing customer or register a new hunter.
    2. Enter harvest information, carcass tag, and hanging weight.
    3. Select services (skinning, caping, aging duration).
    4. Choose cuts, sausage recipes, and fat blend ratios.
    5. Select packaging types and pack portion sizes (1 lb, 2 lb, bulk).
    6. Review the summary, collect an advance deposit, and print the carcass barcode tag.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track shop floor processing and fulfillment">
    Use the **Service Processing** and **Product Processing** queues to advance tasks as your cutting floor completes each operation. Once packaged, the order moves to **Ready to Invoice**, alerts the customer, and prepares for counter pickup.
  </Step>
</Steps>
