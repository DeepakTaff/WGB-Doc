WGB separates processing operations into two distinct stages: **Services** (carcass handling, skinning, caping, aging) and **Products** (steaks, roasts, ground burger, and cured or smoked sausages).

## Service configuration

Services represent labor performed on the whole animal or carcass before final cutting and packaging.

Navigate to **Species Settings** > **Services** to manage your shop's offerings:

### Service parameters
* **Service Name**: e.g., *Standard Processing*, *Shoulder Mount Caping*, *European Skull Mount*, *Hide Tanning Prep*, *Disposal Fee*.
* **Service Color**: Assign color-coded badges (`ServiceColorLU`) that appear on hanging tags and shop floor queue monitors for quick visual sorting.
* **Fee Type**:
  * **Flat Fee**: Fixed dollar charge per animal regardless of weight.
  * **Per Pound Fee**: Calculated based on intake hanging weight or boneless trim weight.
  * **Hourly Fee**: Applied for specialty custom butcher labor.
* **Estimated Turnaround Days**: Default duration required to complete the service.

<Note>
  You can soft-delete or restore services at any time. Active orders retain their historical service rates at the time of check-in.
</Note>

## Products and cut profiles

Products represent consumer cuts and finished meat goods delivered to the customer.

Navigate to **Products Settings** > **Products** to set up available cuts:

### Product constraints and batch minimums
To ensure economical smokehouse and grinder runs, set production thresholds under **Products Settings**:
* **Minimum Batch Weight**: Minimum pounds of boneless meat required to produce a specific sausage variety (e.g., minimum 10 lbs for Jalapeño Cheddar Summer Sausage).
* **Base Price per Pound**: Added to the final invoice based on yield or recipe weight.

## Recipe add-ons and seasonings

Specialty sausages and premium burger blends require ingredients such as fat trimmings, high-temp cheeses, and custom seasonings.

Configure mix-ins in **Products Settings** > **Addons**:

| Add-on Name | Category | Fee Model | Common Use Cases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pork Trim (Fat Blend)** | Fat Supplement | Per Pound | Added to venison at 20% ratio for juicy burgers |
| **Beef Suet (Fat Blend)** | Fat Supplement | Per Pound | Traditional flavor blend for lean game cuts |
| **High-Temp Cheddar** | Mix-in Cheese | Per Pound | Does not melt out during smokehouse cycles |
| **High-Temp Pepperjack** | Mix-in Cheese | Per Pound | Spicy kick for snack sticks and summer sausage |
| **Diced Jalapeños** | Seasoning | Per Pound | Summer sausages and smoked brats |
| **Vacuum Smoke Seasoning** | Cure & Spice | Flat or Per Lb | Specialty jerky and cured meats |

## Packaging types and sizes

Configure packaging styles under **Products Settings** > **Packaging Type** and **Packaging Size**:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Packaging types">
    * **Vacuum Sealing (Heavy Duty 4 Mil)**: Airtight preservation preventing freezer burn for up to 2-3 years. Charged per package or per pound.
    * **Butcher Freezer Paper (Wax-Lined Poly)**: Traditional single-wrapped or double-wrapped white freezer paper. Economical option.
    * **Chub / Ground Meat Bags**: Roll-stock or poly burger bags sealed with hog rings or tape for ground meat.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Packaging sizes">
    Define standardized portion quantities available to customers during order intake:
    * **1 lb Packages**: Ideal for ground meat, steaks (2 per pack), and jerky.
    * **2 lb Packages**: Family-size portions for ground meat, stew meat, and roasts.
    * **Bulk Packages**: 5 lb or 10 lb bags for commercial trim or large gatherings.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
