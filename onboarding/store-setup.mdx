Before processing wild game orders, configure your butcher shop identity and legal billing details in **My Store Settings**.

## Shop identity and branding

Your store logo and business contact details appear across all customer documents, including check-in intake receipts, carcass hanging tags, invoice printouts, and automated email notifications.

### Uploading a shop logo
1. Navigate to **My Store Settings** > **Store Logo**.
2. Drag and drop your shop logo into the upload zone or click **Browse File**.
3. Supported image formats include PNG, JPG, and SVG (maximum size: 5 MB).
4. Click **Save Logo**.

The platform stores your image in secure cloud storage (`ButcherLogo`) and generates optimized variants for both web display and thermal receipt printing.

## Addresses and contact details

WGB maintains separate physical store and billing addresses:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Store physical address">
    Enter the physical location where hunters deliver carcasses and collect processed meat packages:
    * **Street Address**: Physical address line 1 and optional line 2.
    * **City, State, Postal Code**: Validated against geographic lookup tables (`StatesLU`, `CitiesLU`, `PostalCodesLU`).
    * **Contact Phone & Email**: Customer-facing support contact numbers displayed on check-in tags.

    Click **Save Store Address** to commit changes.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Billing address">
    Set the legal entity address used for your Wild Game Butcher platform software subscription invoices:
    * **Company Legal Name**: Registered business entity.
    * **Tax / EIN Number**: Federal employer identification number.
    * **Billing Address Lines**: Address matching your merchant card on file.

    Click **Save Billing Address** to update your account records.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Sales tax configuration

Configure state and municipal sales tax percentages applied to custom processing and retail products:

1. Navigate to **My Store Settings** > **Store Tax Details**.
2. Enter your jurisdiction's tax configuration:
   * **State Tax Rate (%)**: Standard percentage applicable to processing services.
   * **Local / County Tax Rate (%)**: Additional regional surcharges if applicable.
   * **Tax Exemption Flags**: Configure exemptions for specific agricultural or raw meat categories as permitted by local state law.
3. Click **Update Tax Settings**.

The tax rates automatically integrate into the [Invoicing Workflow](/workflows/invoicing-and-payments) when compiling line items.

## Subscription plan management

Manage your platform license tier under **App Settings** > **Subscription Details**:

* **View Plans**: Select between Monthly or Annual billing cycles (`BillingCycle` enum).
* **Promo Code Redemption**: Apply seasonal affiliate or partner discount codes (`verify-promo-code`).
* **Payment Method Updates**: Update credit card and ACH details securely via Stripe Elements.
* **Subscription Invoices**: Review historical software billing invoices and download receipts.
